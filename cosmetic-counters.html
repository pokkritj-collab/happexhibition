COSMETIC COUNTERS & BEAUTY RETAIL

Thailand's beauty-counter builder of choice.

For over twenty years, the world's most demanding beauty houses have trusted Happ Exhibition to manufacture and install their cosmetic counters in Thailand. Chanel has been a partner for more than a decade; Gucci Beauty, Shiseido, Clé de Peau Beauté, Hourglass, Bobbi Brown, Van Cleef & Arpels Parfums and Charlotte Tilbury counters across the country were fabricated in our Pathumthani factories.

A beauty counter is the hardest discipline in retail fabrication: millimetre casework, chemical-resistant finishes that survive alcohol-based product and ten thousand hands, integrated testers and lighting, approvals to each maison's global standard — and installation overnight in an operating mall. That is our home ground, from a single flagship counter to nationwide multi-branch rollouts from Chiang Mai to Khon Kaen to Phuket.

Questions we hear often

Who manufactures cosmetic counters in Thailand?

Happ Exhibition Co., Ltd. is a design-build fabricator founded in 2004, operating three factories (11,300 sqm) in Pathumthani near Bangkok. We manufacture and install cosmetic counters, beauty retail zones and boutiques for international luxury brands, with Chanel as a partner of more than ten years.

What makes Happ Exhibition different from other counter manufacturers?

Precision, and the range of brands that trust it. Our benches build to the millimetre tolerances of international luxury houses — Chanel, Gucci, Shiseido and Van Cleef & Arpels counters come out of our factory under documented five-step QC and LVMH-tier factory audits — and the same craftsmen serve Thai brands like Pañpuri, Pralet and Another Story. Few contractors in Thailand work across both local and international standards at this level of precision.

Which beauty brands has Happ Exhibition built for?

Chanel, Gucci Beauty, Shiseido, Clé de Peau Beauté, Hourglass, Bobbi Brown, Van Cleef & Arpels Parfums, Elixir and Charlotte Tilbury, among others — see the project gallery on this page.

Can you roll out counters to multiple branches nationwide?

Yes. We deliver multi-branch rollouts across Thailand — recent programmes span Bangkok, Nonthaburi, Chiang Mai, Khon Kaen and Phuket — fabricated as repeatable kits in our factory and installed overnight branch by branch.

Do you build to international brand guidelines?

Yes. Most of our work is built directly to each maison's global fixture standard, from approved shop drawings through five-step documented QC, and our factory passes the audit regimes of LVMH-tier houses.
