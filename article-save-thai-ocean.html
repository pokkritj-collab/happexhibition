Save Thai Ocean: ฿73,300 of turtle cookies for cleaner shores

฿73,300 raised

2,410 turtle cookies · trash pick-up equipment · shore cleanups with Koh Tao Clean Up

Save Thai Ocean is our team's ocean-conservation project, run in cooperation with Koh Tao Clean Up. To fund it, the team ran a bake sale with a mascot-worthy product: turtle cookies. 1,205 sets — 2,410 cookies — sold out, raising a total of ฿73,300.

“1,205 sets of turtle cookies became trash pick-up gear and cleanup days on the shore.”

Every baht went to work: the funds bought trash pick-up equipment, and the crew joined Koh Tao Clean Up to host collection activities along the shore. Follow @savethaiocean on Instagram and Facebook to support the next cleanup.
